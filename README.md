# 📊 AtliQ Hardware — Power BI 360° Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

An **end-to-end Power BI Business Intelligence solution** developed for **AtliQ Hardware**, providing a 360° view of business performance across **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive Management**.

The project transforms raw business data into interactive dashboards and actionable insights to support **data-driven decision-making, performance monitoring, and business analysis**.
